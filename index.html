Our mission

Sports open doors.
We make sure they're open to everyone.

Mindset in Motion started in the summer of 2026 with a simple idea: show up for kids who don't always get the chance to play organized sports, and make it the best hour of their week.

We partner with community organizations to run free, high-energy events with games, lessons and races. Kids get to move, compete, laugh and learn that effort and a positive mindset carry over into everything else they do.

We're not here to make money. We're here to make an impact.

⚽

Play

Real games and friendly competition where every kid gets touches on the ball, no matter their skill level.

🧠

Mindset

We celebrate effort, teamwork and getting back up. Those habits matter long after the final whistle.

🤝

Community

We work hand-in-hand with local organizations that already know and serve their kids best.

What an event looks like

One hour. All gas.

  1. 0:00

    Warm-up & welcome

    Names, high-fives, and dynamic stretches to get everyone loose and laughing.

  2. 0:10

    Skills & lessons

    Dribbling, passing and shooting drills in small groups so nobody's standing around.

  3. 0:30

    Races

    Sprints and relay races. Heads up, these get competitive.

  4. 0:45

    Game time

    Small-sided scrimmages to put it all together, then a team huddle to close it out.

FAQ

Good questions

Does it cost anything to participate?

Never. Every Mindset in Motion event is completely free for kids and our partner organizations.

Do kids need experience?

Not at all. Our games and drills are built so total beginners and experienced players can both have fun and get better.

Who do you work with?

So far we've partnered with the Boys & Girls Club of Union. We're looking to work with more community centers, schools and youth programs.

How can I help if I can't volunteer in person?

Donating gently-used equipment (balls, cones, pinnies) and spreading the word both make a big difference.
