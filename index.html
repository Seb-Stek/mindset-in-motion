Our mission

Sports teach kids things a classroom can't: how to compete, how to lose and come back, how to be a good teammate. We want every kid to get that, not just the ones who can pay for it.

Mindset in Motion started in the summer of 2026. The idea was simple: show up for kids who don't usually get to play organized sports, and make it the best hour of their week.

We team up with community organizations to run free events with games, lessons and races. Kids get to move, compete and have fun, and they see that effort and a good attitude carry over into everything else.

We're not trying to make money. We're trying to make a difference.

  1. Play

    Real games and friendly competition. Every kid gets touches on the ball, whatever their skill level.

  2. Mindset

    We celebrate effort, teamwork and getting back up. Those habits stick long after the final whistle.

  3. Community

    We work with local organizations that already know their kids best, and we show up for them.

What an event looks like

About an hour, start to finish. Here's how we usually run it.

  1. 0:00

    Warm-up

    Names, high-fives and stretches to get everyone loose.

  2. 0:10

    Skills

    Dribbling, passing and shooting in small groups, so nobody's standing around.

  3. 0:30

    Races

    Sprints and relays. These get competitive fast.

  4. 0:45

    Games

    Small-sided scrimmages to put it all together, then a team huddle to finish.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Every event is free for the kids and for our partner organizations.

Do kids need experience?

Not at all. Our games and drills work for total beginners and experienced players alike.

Who do you work with?

So far, the Boys & Girls Club of Union. We'd like to work with more community centers, schools and youth programs.

How can I help if I can't come in person?

Donate gently used equipment like balls, cones and pinnies, or tell a friend about us.
