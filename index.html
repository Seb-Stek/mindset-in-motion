Every kid deserves a field to play on

Mindset in Motion teaches kids ages 8 to 12 valuable skills in sports and in everyday life, for free.

Every clinic has two parts. First, kids build real sports skills with hands-on coaching and a fun game. Then we talk about the mental side of the game, and how the mindset they use in sports can help them at school, at home and everywhere else.
