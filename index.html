Every kid deserves a field to play on

Mindset in Motion started in the summer of 2026 with a simple idea: show up for kids who don't usually get to play organized sports, and make it the best hour of their week.

We team up with community organizations to run free events with games, lessons and races. Kids get to move, compete and have fun, and they learn that effort and a good attitude carry over into everything else. We're not trying to make money. We're trying to make a difference.

Play

Real games and friendly competition, where every kid gets touches on the ball.

Mindset

We celebrate effort, teamwork and getting back up after you fall.

Community

We partner with local organizations that already know their kids best.

What an event looks like

About an hour, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Warm-up

    Names, high-fives and stretches to get everyone loose.

  2. 2

    Skills

    Dribbling, passing and shooting in small groups, so nobody's standing around.

  3. 3

    Races

    Sprints, relays and push-up races. These get competitive fast.

  4. 4

    Games

    Small-sided scrimmages, then a team huddle to finish.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Every event is free for the kids and for our partner organizations.

Do kids need experience?

Not at all. Our games and drills work for total beginners and experienced players alike.

Who do you work with?

So far, the Boys & Girls Club of Union County. We'd like to work with more community centers, schools and youth programs.

How can I help if I can't come in person?

Donate gently used equipment like balls, cones and pinnies, or tell a friend about us.
