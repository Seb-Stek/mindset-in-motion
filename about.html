About us

Two friends. One simple idea.

Mindset in Motion is a youth-led nonprofit that brings free sports events to kids in underserved communities. We started in the summer of 2026, and we're just getting going.

Gear in hand at the Russell Triolo Clubhouse

How it started

A lot of kids never get the chance to play organized sports. We wanted to change that, one hour at a time.

In the summer of 2026, two friends who love the game, Sebastian Steketee and Boris, decided to do something about it. The idea was simple: bring the games to the kids, keep it free, and make it the best hour of their week.

They packed up balls, cones and pop-up goals and teamed up with the Boys & Girls Clubs of Union County. Our first event, on August 10, was an hour of soccer games and races outside on the field. One week later we were back, this time inside the gym at the Russell Triolo Clubhouse.

Now we're planning what comes next: more events, more community partners, and more sports.

What we do

We run free, high-energy sports events for kids, in places they already know and trust.

Always free

Every event is free, for the kids and for the organizations that host us. We're not here to make money. We're here to make a difference.

We bring the gear

Balls, cones, pop-up goals, agility hurdles and pinnies. Our hosts just need a field or a gym, and the kids.

Every level welcome

Our games and drills work for first-timers and experienced players alike. Small groups mean nobody's standing around.

Real partners

We team up with community organizations that already know their kids, like the Boys & Girls Clubs of Union County.

What we believe

Three ideas behind everything we do.

Play

Real games and friendly competition, where every kid gets touches on the ball. Sports should be fun first.

Mindset

We celebrate effort, teamwork and getting back up after you fall. Those habits carry over into school and everything else.

Community

We partner with local organizations that already know their kids best, and we keep coming back.

The people behind it

Mindset in Motion is run by young people who love sports and want every kid to get a chance to play.

Our partner

Boys & Girls Clubs of Union County

Both of our events so far have been with the kids at the Boys & Girls Clubs of Union County. For our second event, we took over the gym at their Russell Triolo Clubhouse.

We'd love to work with more organizations: clubs, schools, community centers and youth programs. If your kids would enjoy a free hour of sports, we'll bring everything else.
