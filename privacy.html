Privacy

Your privacy, plainly.

We run free sports clinics. We don't sell anything, we don't run ads, and we don't track you. Here's the short version of what this website collects and why.

Last updated October 13, 2026

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us. When you use the contact form on our Get involved page, we receive your name, your email address, your message, and (if you fill them in) the sport you picked or the organization you're with.

We don't use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or ads, and we don't ask you to make an account.

How we use it

We use what you send only to write back to you and to plan our events. We never sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone for marketing. Messages stay in our email inbox, and we delete them once we no longer need them.

Kids under 13

Our clinics are for kids ages 8 to 12, but this website is meant for parents, guardians, volunteers and partners. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you. If we find out we've received a message from a child under 13 without a parent's permission, we'll delete it.

Photos and videos from our events

We share photos and videos from our clinics to show what we do. We don't post kids' last names, home addresses or schools. If you're a parent or guardian and want a photo or video of your child taken down, email us and we'll remove it from the website as quickly as we can, usually within a few days.

Services this website uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic visit details such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our email. Your message passes through their service on the way to us. See FormSubmit's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts provides the lettering on this site, so your browser asks Google for the font files. See Google's privacy policy.

If you start typing a message and leave the page, your browser keeps a draft on your own device so you don't lose it. That draft never reaches us until you press send, and it's cleared once your message goes through.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have from you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Just email mindsetinmotionkids@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
