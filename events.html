What an event looks like

One hour, split into three parts.

  1. 120 minutes

    Drills

    Hands-on coaching in small groups: ball control, dribbling, passing, shooting and field positioning.

  2. 220 minutes

    Games

    Kids put their new skills to work in a fun game, with friendly competition in a positive, supportive setting.

  3. 320 minutes

    Mindset

    We sit down and talk about the mental side of the game, and how it helps at school, at home and in everyday life.
