Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Every event is free for the kids and for our partner organizations.

Do kids need experience?

Not at all. Our games and drills work for total beginners and experienced players alike.

How long is an event?

About an hour: a warm-up, skills in small groups, races, and games to finish. See how an event works.

What sports do you offer?

Soccer right now. Basketball, tennis and volleyball are on the way, and you can join the list for any of them on our sports page.

Who do you work with?

So far, the Boys & Girls Clubs of Union County. We'd like to work with more community centers, schools and youth programs.

How can I help if I can't come in person?

Donate gently used equipment like balls, cones and pinnies, or tell a friend about us.

How do I reach you?

Use the form above, or email us at mindsetinmotionkids@gmail.com.
